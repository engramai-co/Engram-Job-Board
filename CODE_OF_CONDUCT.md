# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming across issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Critique ideas and implementations, not people. Assume different backgrounds and experience levels; offer useful context without condescension.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, doxxing and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post personal CVs, application records or compensation documents belonging to someone else.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts. Responses should be proportionate to the harm and repeated behavior. A good-faith correction is welcome; retaliation against reporters is not.

Use GitHub's **Report content** menu to report abusive comments or profiles privately to GitHub. For a project-specific intervention, open a minimal issue requesting maintainer contact without naming targets or including sensitive evidence. Do not post private evidence publicly to prove a conduct report. Maintainers will arrange an appropriate channel before requesting details.

These expectations also apply when someone represents the project in public. This policy is maintained by the repository administrators described in [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md).
