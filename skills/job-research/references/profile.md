# Private candidate profile

Reuse known answers; latest instructions win. Store this in an authorized private location, not public sample data.

| Area | Capture |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | Immediate experience, later FT career, specific review, or exceptional alternatives to a satisfactory offer |
| Evidence | Authorized CV/portfolio versions, owned work, tools, contribution and outcomes |
| Active directions | Desired daily work; demonstrated skills versus adjacent experience; paused directions |
| Contract pools | Full-time; Internship/Part-time/Freelance/Volunteer; unknown terms kept separate |
| Timing | Starts, graduation, weekly availability, confirmed versus possible flexibility |
| Geography | Cities, remote eligibility, travel, relocation and secondary locations |
| Constraints | Degree/experience/authorization facts and unknowns; advisory flags versus user-chosen exclusions |
| Platform | Scale/brand preference and why; never a proxy for culture or mentoring |
| Baseline | Optional current role/offer, satisfaction, mandate and separate compensation components |
| Capacity | Batch cap, preparation time, multiple roles at one company |
| Exclusions | Removed employers/roles and paused directions, with stable IDs where possible |
| Permissions | Report versus update; authorized documents/mail; publishing and applying are separate |
| Scoring | Existing model, pool weights, criterion anchors, evidence standards |

Start-date flexibility does not establish legal work eligibility, freelancing permission or employer agreement. Flag questions for appropriate sources. Unresolved eligibility can remain visible without being called confirmed eligible.

## Observable mandate examples

Apply only those relevant to the candidate:

- **Creative production / AI filmmaking:** storytelling, storyboarding, shooting/generation, editing and finished deliverables. Match portfolio and tools; AI marketing copy is not filmmaking.
- **Social / marketing:** distinguish organic content, accounts, PR, paid acquisition and performance analysis; one does not prove all.
- **Production / programmes:** distinguish shoot support, programme research, development, post-production coordination and hands-on producing.
- **Core AI:** model behavior, agents, post-training, evaluation and core AI products versus serving, training platforms and infrastructure.
- **Front-office engineering:** named desk/strategy, direct PM/trader/research collaboration, influence on signals, pricing, execution or decisions. “Support researchers” alone is inconclusive.

Do not award mentoring points from brand or stock recruitment copy. Ask who owns deliverables and how work is divided when unclear.
